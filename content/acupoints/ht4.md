## Location

On the palmar aspect of the forearm, the point is on the radial side of the tendon muscle flexor carpi ulnaris, 1.5 cun above the transverse crease of the wrist.

## Indications

- Cardiac pain, sudden loss of voice.

## Benefits

- Сердечная боль, внезапная потеря голоса.

## Classification

Точка меридиана Сердца (Heart Meridian), точка на меридиане.

## Location

On the medial aspect of the elbow, in the depression between the olecranon of the ulna and the medial epicondyle of the humerus.

## Indications

- Pain in the elbow and arm
- Epilepsy

## Benefits

Активирует меридиан, облегчает боль, очищает жар и сырость-жар.

## Classification

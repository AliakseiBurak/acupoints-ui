## Location

On the perineum, at the midpoint between the root of the scrotum and the anus in males, and at the midpoint between the posterior labial commissure and the anus in females.

## Indications

- Constipation and dysuria, or incontinence of feces and urine, hemorrhoids. prolapse of rectum
- Spermatorrhea, impotence, pruritus vulva
- Asphyxiation from drowning, loss of consciousness, manic psychosis

## Benefits

Питает Инь Почек, устраняет Сырость-Жар, оживляет, лечит выпадение.

## Classification

Точка пересечения сосудов Сцепного (Жэнь-май), Ду-май и Чун-май.

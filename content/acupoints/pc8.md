## Location

In the center of the palm, between the 2nd and 3rd metacarpal bones, closer to the 3rd metacarpal bone. When a fist is made, the point is where the tip of the middle finger touches.

## Indications

- Aphtha (mouth ulcer), halitosis
- Coma, fungal infection of the hand
- Cardiac pain, vomiting

## Benefits

Очищает жар сердца, успокаивает Шэнь.

## Classification

Точка Инь-Источник (Ying-Spring) меридиана Перикарда.

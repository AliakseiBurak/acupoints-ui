## Description

Heart 9, Abbreviated as HT 9, Transliterated Shaochong in Chinese, Lesser Surge in English. On the radial side of the little finger, about 0.1…

**Russian Translation:**

## Location

On the radial side of the little finger, about 0.1 cun posterior to the corner of the nail.

## Indications

- Palpitation, cardiac pain, pain in the chest and hypochondrium
- Manic psychosis, febrile diseases, loss of consciousness

## Benefits

Оживляет, успокаивает Дух (Шэнь), очищает от Жара, усмиряет Ветер.

## Classification

Точка меридиана Сердца (Heart Meridian), точка на меридиане.

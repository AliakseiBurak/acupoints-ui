## Location

On the medial aspect of the upper arm, on the radial side of muscle biceps brachii, 3 cun below the front end of the axillary fossa.

## Indications

- Epistaxis (nosebleed)
- Cough, asthma
- Pain in the shoulder and medial aspect of the upper arm.
- Psychological issues such as depression, dizziness, forgetfulness and confusion (Window of the Sky point).

## Benefits

Опускает Ци Лёгких, останавливает кровотечение, успокаивает По.

## Classification

Точка «Окно Неба» (Window of the Sky), точка меридиана Лёгких.

## Location

On the ulnar aspect of the wrist, in the depression between the styloid process of the ulna and the triquetral bone.

## Indications

- Neck pain, pain of the hand and wrist
- Febrile diseases

## Benefits

Очищает жар, очищает меридиан, уменьшает отёк.

## Classification

Точка Цзин-Река (Jing-River) меридиана Тонкого кишечника.
